# Code of conduct

Be direct about the work and respectful toward the person doing it.

Disagreement is welcome. Harassment, threats, personal attacks, discriminatory language,
doxxing, and sexualized conduct are not. Respect privacy, accept a clear request to stop,
and keep reviews about evidence and tradeoffs.

Report conduct concerns privately to `hello@afternote.dev`. Reports will be handled as
confidentially as practical. Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions, restrict
participation, or ban contributors when behavior makes the project unsafe or unproductive.

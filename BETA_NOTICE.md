## Backups and checking results

Keep independent backups of information you need. Afternote Local is not a backup
service, and the maintainer cannot recover your local vault or its encryption
key for you. Exports contain plaintext; protect them and any backups yourself.

Check recalled notes and AI answers against their sources before relying on them.
Assess whether the beta and each connected AI tool meet your needs and any
workplace requirements before saving confidential or regulated information.
See the [security model](docs/SECURITY_MODEL.md) for technical boundaries.

## Privacy and connected tools

Local does not require an Afternote account. Its vault and search run on your Mac,
and the Local app has no app usage telemetry. Approved AI tools can receive
note content and metadata within their granted access; those tools may send it
to their providers. Their terms, retention, and privacy practices apply to those
copies. Revoking a connection does not erase information it already received.

Official builds check GitHub's signed update feed daily by default. You can turn
automatic checks off in Settings. Update requests do not send notes or an optional
system profile, but GitHub receives ordinary network request information.
Website analytics and information you voluntarily send for support are separate
from Local app usage.

Read the [Local privacy notice](https://afternote.dev/privacy#local) for details.
The original hosted service has [separate terms](https://afternote.dev/terms#hosted)
and [privacy information](https://afternote.dev/privacy#hosted).
